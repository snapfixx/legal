  • No contacts list, no call log, no SMS, no microphone, no camera roll beyond the photographs you choose to upload for a listing.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

3. Location — exactly how it is used

Location is the most sensitive thing the app touches, so here it is in full.

  • Your browser asks your permission first. You can refuse, and the app keeps working — you type your address instead.
  • We read your location only when you tap a button: "Use my current location" when booking or signing up as a technician, and "I have started work" on a job.
  • We store the coordinates and a text version of the address.
  • To turn coordinates into a readable address we send them to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service, a free third-party service. It receives the coordinates. If that lookup fails we keep the numbers and lose only the words.
  • There is no background location, no continuous tracking, and no location history.

5. Why we are allowed to hold it

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you sign in and when you fill in a form, for the specific purposes listed in section 2. You may withdraw your consent at any time — see section 7. Withdrawing it means we can no longer provide the parts of SnapFixx that depend on that data.

7. Your rights, and how to use them

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask us to:

  • Show you what personal data we hold about you, and a summary of how it is processed
  • Correct anything inaccurate, incomplete or out of date — much of this you can do yourself inside the app, including your name, phone, location pin, skills and shop name
  • Erase your personal data, where we are not required to keep it
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapable
  • Raise a grievance with us, before going to the Data Protection Board of India

How to ask

Email snapfixxsupport@gmail.com from the email address on your SnapFixx account, telling us what you want. We will acknowledge within 24 hours and respond within 15 days.

One honest limit on erasure. Some records cannot be deleted immediately — completed orders, invoices and warranty records must be kept for the period in section 6, because tax law and your own warranty claim both depend on them. We will delete everything we are not required to keep, and tell you plainly what is left and why.

8. Children

SnapFixx is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to snapfixxsupport@gmail.com and we will remove it.

9. How we protect your data

  • Sign-in is handled by Google. We never see or store your password.
  • Access to the database is controlled by server-side security rules, so that a person can read their own bookings and chats and not somebody else's. A technician can see a job only once it has been assigned to them.
  • A technician account is inactive until the office approves it. Until then it sees no customer data at all.
  • Listings are reviewed before they appear in the Store.
  • Chat blocks the sharing of phone numbers and email addresses.

No system is perfect. If a breach affects your personal data we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
